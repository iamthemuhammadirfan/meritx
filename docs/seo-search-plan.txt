What the owner should do next
1. Add meritxsc.com as a domain property in Google Search Console and complete Google's DNS verification.
2. Submit https://meritxsc.com/sitemap.xml; use URL Inspection to review the main service and laptop pages.
3. Confirm or claim the Google Business Profile using the real business identity and verified address/contact details.
4. Provide real projects, approved installation photos, buyer questions and referenceable outcomes so the site can gain original evidence over time.
5. If a product catalogue is wanted, provide actual models, current prices, stock and warranty terms. Product/Offer data should reflect genuine purchasable products, not generic illustrative images.
6. Review Search Console query, impression, click and indexing data after Google has had time to process the changes. Improve pages from that evidence.

No code change or animation can guarantee first place for “buy laptops” or any other query. Specific business and local intent is a more useful starting point for this supplier than presenting an unverified retail catalogue.
